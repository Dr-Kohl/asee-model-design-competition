Toronto Food Run

2027 ASEE Model Design Competition · Toronto, Ontario · Monday, June 21

Current competition

Design an autonomous Toronto food delivery system

Teams deliver four food items to assigned customer addresses and complete the poutine delivery at the CN Tower Bowl. Every team receives four 120-second autonomous trials.
